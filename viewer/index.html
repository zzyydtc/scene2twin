    Labels and confidences are inferences from the reconstructed points. They were checked against a laser scan on these two scenes only. Registering the reconstruction to that scan did not work, so no scan geometry is shown here.

    Tape distances used for scale were taken from the laser scan to simulate measuring a real room.

    The photo overlay shows only the keypoints that were triangulated into 3D points. "View from here" ignores lens distortion and levels the camera roll, so it is approximate.

    Points come from COLMAP structure-from-motion on ETH3D photos. Code and results: scene2twin. Photos and derived data: ETH3D (Schöps et al., CVPR 2017), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
